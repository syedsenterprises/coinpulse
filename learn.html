Learn, in plain words

What is a blockchain?

A shared record book that nobody owns alone. Every transaction is written in blocks, chained together, and copied to thousands of computers.

What is a wallet?

Not a place coins sit — a set of keys. The public key is your address; the private key is the password you must never share.

Why do prices swing?

Supply, demand, news, regulation and speculation. Crypto moves faster than stocks because the market never closes and is smaller.

Risk, honestly

You can lose everything you put in. Never invest borrowed money, and never invest money you need for rent or food.

Glossary

HODL — holding through volatility. Gas — the fee for using a network. Staking — locking coins to help secure a network, for rewards. DeFi — financial services without a bank in the middle. Market cap — price × circulating supply. Volatility — how much and how fast a price moves. ATH — all-time high price. Bear/bull market — falling/rising market trend.
